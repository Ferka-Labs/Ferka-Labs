## About

Ferka Labs is a software studio focused on designing and building digital products from the ground up — websites, internal tools, and applications — for businesses and for our own ventures.

We work as a small, focused team rather than a large agency: fewer handoffs, direct ownership of the code, and a preference for clean, deliberate design over templated defaults.

## What We Build

- **Web platforms** — corporate sites, digital catalogs, and admin dashboards.
- **Custom systems** — inventory, sales tracking, and internal business tools.
- **Automation** — workflow and messaging integrations (WhatsApp, APIs, webhooks).
- **Applications** — mobile and desktop products, built and maintained in-house.

Every product ships under the Ferka Labs name, built with the same attention to detail whether it's client work or one of our own apps.
